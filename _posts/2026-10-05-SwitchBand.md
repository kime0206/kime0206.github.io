Description of Switch Band Project:
This switch band includes two "switches" in order to complete the circuit and make the pink heart light up. The first component is a simple snap, which doubles as a nice clasp
in order to hold the switch band in place. The second one is in the form of a movable star block (flipping it left will turn the LED off; right will turn it on). The overall design
is a childlike design with two depicted bunnies.

Tip for Beginners:
When attempting this project for the first time, make sure that you have enough room on your switch band in order to create breaks in the circuit in both the 
negative and the positive lines. Otherwise you'll have to go around everything and make your life 10x harder...
