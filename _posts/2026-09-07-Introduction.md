Hi! My name is Eunchae, and I am a senior studying Political Science and Studio Art. I have taken sewing classes (both machine and hand) when I was in elementary school but haven't delved into anything more professional than that. I create lots of crafts that involve hand sewing, but I mainly end up hemming shirts and loose buttons for my parents. My circuitry/Arduino experience is more limited, providing for a steeper learning curve in that area. However, I have taken summer camps in which we learned basic circuitry (if that counts at all). Through this eTextiles class, I hope to expand my world of the intersection between art and technology. I am always looking for more ways to grow as an artist, and I think that introducing technology into the mix would be the perfect way to do just that. To close out, my boring fact is that I love to collect stickers. Thank you :)
