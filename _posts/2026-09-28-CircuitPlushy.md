Description of Plushy Project:
I created a purple plushy with led eyes and heart that lights up when its heart wand makes contact with its hand. The back flap is for easy replacement of the battery.

Tip for Beginners:
If your circuit isn't working, make sure that your conductive thread doesn't overlap! Put pieces of fabric in between the threads to create a barrier between the
positive and the negative sides.
