Description of Plushy Project:
I created a purple plushy with led eyes and heart that lights up when its heart wand makes contact with its hand. The back flap is for easy replacement of the battery. There is also a slide switch inserted inside, and I used conductive fabric in order to create my second "break" in the circuit, replacing the button component of this project.

Tip for Beginners:
If your circuit isn't working, make sure that your conductive thread doesn't overlap! Put pieces of fabric in between the threads to create a barrier between the positive and the negative sides.
